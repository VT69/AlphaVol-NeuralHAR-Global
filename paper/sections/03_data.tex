\section{Data}
\label{sec:data}
We analyze three structurally distinct assets: Bitcoin (BTC), the S\&P 500 index (SPX), and the NIFTY 50 index (NIFTY). Table \ref{tab:desc_stats} presents the descriptive statistics for the daily log realized volatility of each asset. The data spans from January 23, 2019, to July 28, 2026, for BTC ($N=2,744$), and from February 5, 2015, to July 28, 2026, for SPX ($N=2,862$) and NIFTY ($N=2,755$). For BTC, realized variance is computed using 5-minute intraday prices; for the equity indices, a realized volatility proxy is constructed from daily OHLCV data.

The time series of realized volatility for all three assets visually exhibits the stylized facts of financial volatility, including volatility clustering and a pronounced, synchronous spike during the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic in early 2020. 

In addition to realized volatility, our feature matrix includes a suite of exogenous variables. Market microstructure is captured via Volume-Synchronized Probability of Informed Trading (VPIN), Order Book Imbalance (OBI), and Amihud Illiquidity. Macroeconomic controls include the term slope and credit spread. Finally, sentiment is measured via a daily aggregate FinBERT score derived from news headlines, a calculated sentiment surprise metric, and retail fear/greed indices.
